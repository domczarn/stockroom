## Description
Never type a Vinted sale again.

Set up one email rule and Stockroom does the rest: every Vinted sale, shipping label, QR code and delivery arrives by itself, matched to your stock, with your real profit worked out. No Vinted login, no bots, nothing that can get your account banned.

AT THE COUNTER
Your parcel QR codes are ready before you walk in, even with no signal. Tap "Post a parcel" on your home screen, scan, swipe to the next one.

KNOW YOUR REAL PROFIT
What you paid, what you sold it for, every fee and cost. Last 24 hours, 7 days, 30 days or 90 days.

HMRC, SORTED
See how close you are to Vinted reporting you to HMRC (30 sales or about £1,700 a year), and get a Self Assessment pack in one tap.

PIP, YOUR RESELLING SIDEKICK
• Writes your Vinted listing from your photos
• Tells you what to charge, and how fast similar pieces sell
• Buy or pass: snap something in a charity shop, see the likely profit before you buy

Works with Vinted, eBay and Depop sale emails, and the couriers Vinted uses (Evri, Royal Mail, InPost and more).

Free: 20 items and 20 auto-tracked sales a month. Pro: everything unlimited.
