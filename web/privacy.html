Booked & Paid Stockroom

Privacy policy

Last updated 21 November 2026

Who we are

Stockroom is run by Booked & Paid Ltd, a company registered in the UK. We are the controller of the personal data described here. Contact us at info@bookednpaid.com.

What we collect

  • Account: your email address, sign-in sessions (the device type and when you signed in) and passkey public keys. We never see your fingerprint or face data.
  • Your workspace: the stock, purchases, sales, expenses, orders, notes and photos you add.
  • Profile: an optional username and your choices about what you share on the leaderboard and with friends, plus messages you send to friends.
  • Billing: your plan and Stripe customer reference. Card details are handled by Stripe and never reach us.
  • Gmail (optional): see “Gmail data” below.
  • Technical: basic request logs (time, address, errors) kept by our hosting provider to run and secure the service.

How we use it

To run Stockroom for you: store your workspace, sign you in, take payments, send sign-in codes and the optional weekly report, power Pip’s features, keep the service secure and answer support requests. Our legal basis is performing our contract with you, and our legitimate interest in keeping Stockroom secure and working. We don’t sell your data, show ads or use your data to train AI models.

Pip’s AI features

When you use Ask Pip, Write with Pip, the offer helper, Buy or pass, screenshot reading or the weekly report, the relevant item details, figures from your workspace and any photo you choose are sent to our AI provider (Anthropic, or Cloudflare Workers AI as a fallback) to produce the answer. Photo studio sends the photo to Cloudflare Images to remove the background. These providers process the data on our behalf and don’t use it to train their models.

Who we share it with

  • Cloudflare: hosting, database, photo storage, background removal and fallback AI.
  • Anthropic: Claude, for Pip’s AI features.
  • Stripe: payments and subscriptions.
  • Resend: sending sign-in codes, invites and weekly reports.
  • Google: only if you connect Gmail.

Some of these providers process data outside the UK. Where they do, they use safeguards recognised under UK data protection law, such as the UK International Data Transfer Addendum.

Gmail data

Connecting Gmail is optional and separate from signing in. You choose which Google account to connect. Stockroom requests read-only access to find Vinted emails. It stores the connected address, an encrypted refresh token, and for each Vinted email: the message ID, subject, a short preview (snippet), received date, category and your review state. It does not copy full email bodies or attachments, send emails, change labels or mark messages read. Details from these emails (such as item names and sale amounts) can update your workspace and may be included when you use Pip’s AI features.

You can pause checks or disconnect in Settings. Disconnecting removes the saved credential and email summaries. You can also revoke access in your Google Account. Stockroom’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We don’t sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or use it to train AI models.

Cookies and storage

We use one essential cookie to keep you signed in. Your device also stores a copy of your workspace and your preferences so the app works offline. There are no advertising or tracking cookies. Signing out clears this device’s copy.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account is open. Delete your account any time in Settings → Data & backups: your workspace, photos, sign-ins and profile are deleted straight away, and database backups roll off within 30 days. We keep records of payments for as long as UK tax law requires, and a short security log of account actions.

Your rights

You can access, export (Settings → Data & backups), correct or delete your data, object to or restrict how we use it, and complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ico.org.uk). Email us to use any of these rights.

Changes

If we make important changes we’ll tell you in the app or by email before they apply.
