Gmail data & privacy

Connecting Gmail is optional and separate from signing in to Stockroom. You choose which Google account to connect. Stockroom requests read-only Gmail access to find Vinted order emails and present a review inbox for resale tracking.

Stockroom searches a recent window for the Vinted sender and reads matching message metadata. It stores the connected address, an encrypted refresh token, the message ID, subject, received date, category and your review state. It does not copy email bodies or attachments, send emails, change Gmail labels, or mark your Gmail messages read.

Your connection and receipt summaries are available in your own signed-in Stockroom. Optional device notifications contain a generic update. We use Google user data to provide this feature. We do not sell it, use it for advertising, or use it to train AI models.

In Vinted updates, you can pause checks or disconnect. Disconnecting removes the saved credential and receipt summaries from Stockroom. You can also revoke access in your Google Account’s third-party connections.

Stockroom’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.
