Questions

Good to know.

Does Stockroom post on Vinted for me?

No. Stockroom never logs in to or automates your Vinted account. Pip writes the listing and you copy it in, which keeps your account safe.

Do I need to connect Gmail?

No, it’s optional. If you connect it, Stockroom reads only Vinted emails about things you bought, sold and posted, and uses them to update your stock.

Is Pip always right?

Pip is very good but not perfect. Its listings, prices and flaw checks are suggestions to help you decide, so give them a quick check before you post.

Where is my data kept?

In your own private workspace, hosted on Cloudflare. You can export a full backup or delete your account any time from Settings.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. Stockroom is built phone-first. Add it to your home screen and it works like an app.
